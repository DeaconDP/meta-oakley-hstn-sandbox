# DAT capabilities → blind and low-vision (BLV) features on HSTN

2026-10-22. Builds on `notes/hstn-capabilities.md` (the capability values are there and aren't repeated here) and `notes/bench-android-native.md`. Sources: DAT docs v1.0 via the meta-wearables-docs MCP, the Android skills under `A/plugins/mwdat-android/skills/`, and the iOS submodule. Short forms are the same as in the capabilities note: `A/`, `I/`, `docs/` = `https://wearables.developer.meta.com/docs/`. **Platform** marks Android/iOS OS behaviour that the DAT docs don't cover. It's unverified on glasses.

## Summary

- Meta names accessibility as an encouraged use case: "**Assistive and accessibility** — scene description, captioning, live translation, navigation aid." The design principle it gives is "**narrow purpose, short retention, obvious to bystanders**" (`docs/develop/dat/acceptable-use` > What we encourage). The DAT docs say nothing about Be My Eyes, blindness, TalkBack or VoiceOver. I searched the corpus and the iOS and Android submodules.
- **A GA-only BLV app is possible today.** It needs the camera stream, in-stream `stream.capturePhoto()` and device state. All three are GA. Only `Stream.audioStream` is tagged dev/beta on `Stream`, and standalone `camera.photo` is the experimental photo path (`docs/reference/android/dat/1.0/com_meta_wearable_dat_camera_stream`). All audio input and output stays plain OS Bluetooth.
- Everything that would make it hands-free is **Experimental**: on-glasses ASR, Inputs (capture button), voice invocation and Motion. These can go to the dev and Beta channels but not to production.
- Mock Device Kit (MDK) can drive every DAT capability except in-stream mic audio, which has no frame injection. It can't simulate glasses audio routing at all.
- The biggest BLV risks are UX ones. **A one-finger tap pauses the session and tap-and-hold stops it** (`docs/develop/dat/inputs` > Interactions reserved by the device). A blind user who brushes the temple can kill the session without knowing, so every session and stream state change has to be announced aloud.

## Capability → BLV features

| Capability | BLV features enabled | Status | MDK? | Constraints that bite |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Camera stream | Live scene description, "what am I facing", object/person finder (generic "a person", not identity), door/sign/text detector, **low-vision magnifier on the phone**: zoom, contrast and invert filters on the HIGH 720x1280 feed | GA | Yes: `setCameraFeed(Uri)` or `setCameraFeed(CameraFacing)` uses the **phone camera** as the glasses feed, which is good for real OCR and detection tests | Bluetooth Classic ladder: drops one resolution step, then fps, never below 15. Per-frame compression can make HIGH look soft, and a lower resolution or fps can look sharper (`docs/develop/dat/streaming` > Framerate and resolution). Mock first frame is 975 ms median. iOS `raw` frames stop in the background; `hvc1` keeps delivering (`docs/reference/ios_swift/dat/1.0/mwdatcamera_videocodec`). The docs don't say how Android behaves in the background. Errors: `HINGE_CLOSED`, `THERMAL_HOT`, `BATTERY_LOW`, `PEAK_POWER_LIMIT` (`.../com_meta_wearable_dat_camera_types_streamerror`) |
| In-stream photo (`stream.capturePhoto()`) | OCR/read-aloud of documents, labels and mail; "describe in detail" stills for a VLM | GA (not tagged dev/beta) | Yes: `setCapturedImage` | Only works while the stream is active, one capture in flight at a time (`CaptureInProgress`). Mock is **3940 ms median** to a decoded 960x1440 bitmap. The stream frame is faster but worse for OCR |
| Standalone photo (`camera.photo`) | Max-detail OCR at FULL 4032x3024 (small print, medication labels) | Experimental | Yes: `setCapturedPhoto`, `simulateCaptureFailure` | Competes with the stream: stop the stream first. Photo is terminal after `stop()`, so remove and re-add the Camera to capture again (`camera-capture` skill). Has a `transferProgressStream` to announce progress. No real-hardware latency number yet |
| Stream mic audio (PCM in-band) | Ambient-sound awareness (alarms, traffic, a knock), audio+frame "what's that noise", sending the wearer's question to a cloud STT/LLM **without leaving A2DP** | Experimental | Negotiates only. "Do not assume mock PCM frames will arrive" (`audio-streaming` skill) | Not beamformed, so ambient sound comes through at natural levels. Keeps output on A2DP, which is the reason to use it (`docs/develop/dat/microphones-and-speakers` > Capture audio while streaming). 16/44.1/48 kHz. Needs `Permission.MICROPHONE` plus Camera |
| HFP mic (OS) | Voice commands and Q&A to your own STT/LLM; beamforming isolates the wearer in noisy streets | OS-level, shippable | No (no BT on the emulator; the phone mic stands in) | **Mutually exclusive with A2DP**: output drops to 16/8 kHz mono for the whole session (`> Choose a profile`). iOS: settle the HFP route about 2 s before starting the DAT stream, or audio "can fail silently" (`> iOS > HFP`). Mic permission goes through the OS dialog, not Meta AI (`docs/develop/dat/build-overview` > Integration lifecycle) |
| Speakers A2DP/HFP (OS) | All spoken output: descriptions, OCR read-out, earcons and spatial-ish beeps for the finder, state announcements | OS-level, shippable | No: TTS falls back to `route=phone` on the emulator (bench) | No DAT audio API. A2DP is 44.1/48 kHz stereo, HFP is mono voice. Verify the route before assuming glasses output (`> iOS > Verify the route`). Coexisting with screen readers is **Platform** (see Audio routing) |
| On-glasses ASR (`Speech`) | Hands-free commands ("read this", "what's ahead", "where's the door"), no cloud STT | Experimental | Yes: `simulateTranscription(text, isFinal, confidence)`, plus live phone recognition with `RECORD_AUDIO` | Act only on `isFinal`. `confidence = -1` means no score, not low confidence (`docs/develop/dat/speech` > Overview). Locale reported after start. Whether it holds HFP/A2DP or contends with the stream is undocumented |
| IMU (`Motion`) | Head-pointing "what am I facing" (gate the description on a still head), head-tilt for "look down to read", tilt/head-drop alert, **heuristic** fall alert, camera-stability gating before capture (fewer blurry OCR shots) | Experimental | Yes: `setMotionFeed(samples, loop)` or a CSV | 5–60 Hz, 256-sample buffer drops the oldest. **Magnetometer "currently unavailable ... on Ray-Ban Meta"** (`.../com_meta_wearable_dat_motion_types_motionsample`). HSTN shares V128, so expect no glasses compass. BirdSpotter falls back to the phone compass (`A/samples/BirdSpotter/.../data/location/FailoverAim.kt`) and measures pitch from gravity (`GlassesAim.kt`). A fall detector at a 60 Hz cap with no compass is a demo, not a safety device |
| Inputs | Physical, non-visual triggers: capture button `SHORT_PRESS` = describe, `HOLD` = read text, captouch double-tap `Select` = repeat, swipe `Nav` = next/previous sentence | Experimental | Yes: `input.navDown()`, Select, Back, Button, Capture, Drag | Single tap (pause) and tap-and-hold (stop) are reserved and never reach the app. Mapping is decided by the device and swipe direction can be inverted (`docs/develop/dat/inputs` > Input sources). HSTN `ACTION_BUTTON` is unconfirmed |
| Voice invocation | "Hey Meta, start {app}" cold-launches straight into describe mode, so the user never has to find the phone | Experimental | Yes: `simulateLaunchAppAction()` | Must `sendSuccess`/`sendFailure` exactly once. Avoid "AI" in the app name (`docs/develop/dat/voice-invocations` > Best practices). Needs Developer Center approval |
| Device state | "Glasses removed": auto-pause and say so. "Glasses folded": announce that the session ended. Spoken battery level at thresholds (50/20/10), charging, thermal warnings ("glasses are hot, switching to low quality"), works before a session starts | GA | Yes: `setBatteryLevel`, `setChargingState`, `setThermalLevel`, `don`/`doff`, `fold`/`unfold` (`docs/develop/dat/device-state` > Test without hardware) | Every enum starts `UNKNOWN` and may stay that way. Values go stale on disconnect. Thermal `MODERATE` = cut resolution, `SEVERE` = shed load, `CRITICAL+` = device may vanish (`> Respond to thermal level`). Android battery 0 = unknown or empty |

## Cross-cutting constraints

| Topic | What the docs say / what we know | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Audio: A2DP vs HFP | Exclusive. Opening HFP for the mic degrades **all** output, TTS and screen reader included, to mono voice quality. Best BLV combination: in-stream PCM (Exp.) or on-glasses `Speech` (Exp.) for input, with A2DP kept for output. GA-only combination: HFP for input and accept mono output, or use the phone mic | `docs/develop/dat/microphones-and-speakers` > Overview, > Choose a profile |
| Audio: TalkBack / VoiceOver coexistence | **Not in the DAT docs.** Platform: screen-reader speech follows the active route, so it goes to the glasses on A2DP and to mono SCO once in HFP/`MODE_IN_COMMUNICATION`. Android: give app TTS `USAGE_ASSISTANCE_ACCESSIBILITY` and transient focus with `MAY_DUCK` so it doesn't fight TalkBack. iOS: prefer `UIAccessibility.post(.announcement)` so VoiceOver speaks in the user's own voice and rate, or use `AVSpeechSynthesizer` with `.duckOthers`. Avoid two voices talking at once (queue or interrupt policy). Verify on hardware | Platform |
| Latency (mock) | session_start 221 ms, first frame 975 ms, capturePhoto→bitmap **3940 ms**, TTS start 211 ms. A "describe" round trip on mock is roughly 4.2 s plus model time. Use the latest stream frame for a fast answer and `capturePhoto` for detail | `notes/bench-android-native.md` |
| Bandwidth ladder | Bluetooth Classic, automatic, floor 15 fps. For OCR: lower fps (2–7) at HIGH, or a still photo | `docs/develop/dat/streaming` > Framerate and resolution |
| Privacy LED / bystanders | **The DAT docs don't mention the capture LED** (searched). The only bystander rule in the docs is the principle "obvious to bystanders" and "clear on-site notice" for enterprise. Binding rules are in the AUP and developer terms, which aren't in the MCP corpus. Read them before any person-identification feature. Meta reviews apps and can revoke capabilities "without prior notice" | `docs/develop/dat/acceptable-use` > What we encourage, > Before you build, > We review and we enforce |
| AI use | The docs don't restrict sending frames or audio to an AI service. "Meta does not access photos/videos or audio transferred directly ... to the third-party mobile app", so retention and consent are the app's job. The permissions tab needs a justification for camera and mic | `docs/develop/dat/build-overview` > Overview; `docs/develop/dat/manage-projects` > Permissions |
| Screen off / background | iOS: `raw` frames stop when backgrounded; `hvc1` keeps delivering. Android: undocumented. Expect a foreground service; the bench hasn't tested it. Blind users often run with the screen off (or VoiceOver Screen Curtain), so this is a must-test | `docs/reference/ios_swift/dat/1.0/mwdatcamera_videocodec` |
| Hinges / removal / tap | Hinge closed → Bluetooth disconnect, stream `HINGE_CLOSED`, session `STOPPED` (terminal; reopening doesn't restart it, so `createSession` again). Doff → pause "when wear detection is enabled". One-finger tap → `PAUSED`/resume, tap-and-hold → stop. Motion auto-resumes after a pause on Android | `docs/develop/dat/build-overview` > Sessions; `docs/develop/dat/lifecycle-events`; `motion` skill |
| One session per device | "Certain features are unavailable while your session is active." It doesn't say which. That matters if the user also relies on Meta AI's own assistive features | `docs/develop/dat/build-overview` > Sessions |
| Gating | Capabilities can be refused at runtime by a server-side gate (`CAPABILITY_DENIED`) whatever the versions | `docs/develop/dat/version-dependencies` > Versions alone do not guarantee a capability |
| Distribution | Experimental → dev and private Beta channel only (one channel, invite by email, tester cap shown in the Users tab). Developer Mode allows **one third-party app registered at a time** | `docs/develop/dat/set-up-release-channels`; `docs/develop/dat/permissions-requests` > Distribution and registration |

## Open questions that need real HSTN hardware

1. Does phone TTS (and TalkBack/VoiceOver) actually route to the HSTN speakers over A2DP while a DAT stream is running, and what's the speech-start latency on glasses?
2. Real `capturePhoto` and FULL standalone photo latency, and real first-frame time, over Bluetooth Classic. Is OCR on HIGH stream frames usable at 7 fps vs 30?
3. Android with screen off or locked: do frames, `Speech` and Motion keep flowing (with a foreground service), or does the session pause?
4. Does `Speech` or in-stream PCM force HFP, contend with A2DP output, or conflict with an active camera stream?
5. Does HSTN report `magnetometer` and `orientation`, or are they null as on Ray-Ban Meta? (BirdSpotter's `GlassesAim.kt` comment cites a 53 µT reading on "a worn pair", which conflicts with the reference note.)
6. Is wear detection on by default, and how fast does `DOFFED` arrive? Does doff pause or stop on HSTN?
7. HSTN input surfaces: is there an `ACTION_BUTTON`? Exact capture-button press mapping? Can a BLV user reliably avoid the reserved single-tap and hold?
8. Which Meta AI features (including any built-in assistive ones) become unavailable while a DAT session is active?
9. Capture LED behaviour during DAT stream vs photo, so a demo script can tell bystanders accurately.
10. Is there any server-side `CAPABILITY_DENIED` for Experimental capabilities on HSTN firmware V128 with Meta AI V292?
